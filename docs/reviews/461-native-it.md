# Native read (Italian, Ticino): UI messages `messages/it.json` (#461)

Reviewer: native Italian speaker from Ticino, Swiss rental practice. Read at commit a4a7ebcf (worktree `review-461-native`, read only, nothing changed): every key of `messages/it.json` (1770 lines) side by side with `messages/de.json`, checked against `docs/design/i18n-begriffe.md` (binding terms of #405 and the style rules) and `docs/reviews/w4d-native-it.md` (PDF and letters, not repeated here).

Overall: the Italian is correct, in the right register and uses the binding terms consistently. The words that just changed are applied everywhere: «spese amministrative», «grado di certezza», «annullare la disdetta», «inquilino sostitutivo». None of «spese di gestione», «affidabilità», «revocare» or «assicurazione edificio» is left. Typography is clean: «» without spaces, typographic apostrophe ’, «…», no straight quotes. The three Major findings below are a button in informal «tu» (against the Lei/infinitive rule), one help text whose idiom says the wrong thing, and one help text whose conjunction gives the opposite meaning. The rest is polish, consistency and a few grammar slips.

## Not confirmed

- **Art. 264 cpv. 1 CO, Italian wording (IT-8).** From memory, the Italian text reads «… un nuovo conduttore solvibile e che il locatore possa ragionevolmente accettare». I could not open Fedlex from this read-only review. If the official wording is «non possa ragionevolmente rifiutare», keep the current ending and fix only the missing subject («se l’inquilino Le propone …») and the subjunctive.
- **Courtesy capitals (IT-20).** I propose lowercase possessives and clitics because they are the majority in the UI and the convention of the PDF and letters (w4d review). Swiss federal Italian style guides also accept capitals («La», «Le», «Suo») in correspondence. Which one Dwello uses is a style decision for the product owner; either is fine if it is applied throughout.
- **Binding terms: no product-owner question.** None is plainly wrong. Note on «annullare la disdetta» (#405): in the CO, «annullare/annullabilità della disdetta» (art. 271, 273 CO) is the remedy before the conciliation authority or the court. The help text (`contracts_withdraw_notice_help`) also covers a joint withdrawal («ritirare insieme»), so the button is acceptable and reads naturally; I did not flag it. «spese amministrative», «grado di certezza» and «inquilino sostitutivo» are idiomatic and applied consistently.
- **`contracts_billed_locked_*`, `contracts_billed_draft_*` («date di contratto»).** I kept «date» there because the context is a changed end date (IT-22 covers only the two keys where «Vertragsdaten» means details in general). I did not check every code path that shows these messages.
- **IT-36 and the quote in `contracts_statements_missing_text`.** I assume the button label is shown as «Allestire {period}»; I did not open the page to see whether the quoted «Allestire» must match exactly.
- **Master data names** (categories, keys, profiles) come from the database (`name_it`), not from `messages/it.json`, and were outside this read; their earlier findings stay in the w4d review (IT-M1 to IT-M3).
