# Native French read (Romandie): «Abgerechnet bis» / «Décompté jusqu’au» (#453)

Reader: native French speaker from Romandie, Swiss rental practice, reading as a tenant (PDF) and as a landlord (app texts). Read at commit fdd72b0d: `native-453/abgerechnet-bis-fr.pdf` / `.txt` (compared with `abgerechnet-bis-de.txt`), the `fr` keys `tenancyLine`, `tenancyBilledLine`, `advancePaymentsFrom`, `heatingMonthsBilled`, `partProfileBilled` in `backend/src/services/pdf.service.ts`, and nine keys in `messages/fr.json`, each compared with the German. Binding terms are kept («décompte», «Décompté jusqu’au», «jours de location», «acompte»).

Overall: a tenant in Romandie understands the PDF. It is clear that everything up to 29.02.2024 was settled in an earlier statement, and that the advance payments count from 01.03.2024. The register (vous) and the legal references are right. In the source, every space before «:» «;» «%» and inside « » is U+00A0 (` ` in pdf.service.ts, a literal U+00A0 in fr.json), so the typography is right and WinAnsi-safe. `pdftotext` shows those spaces as ordinary spaces, so the sample `.txt` cannot confirm them. What is left is idiom: the sentence repeats «décomptés dans un décompte», «couvre» has no article before the date range, «mois décomptés» can read as «months deducted» (in everyday French, «décompter» also means to subtract), and the app says «saisi pour le contrat» where French would say «saisi dans».
