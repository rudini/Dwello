# Native English read: «Abgerechnet bis» (#453)

Reader: native English speaker, tenant in Switzerland. Read at commit fdd72b0d: the rendered sample `abgerechnet-bis-en.pdf` (txt) against the German one; `pdf.service.ts` en keys `tenancyLine`, `tenancyBilledLine`, `advancePaymentsFrom`, `heatingMonthsBilled`, `partProfileBilled`; the listed `messages/en.json` keys against `de.json`.

Overall: no Blocker or Major. A tenant understands the statement. The tenancy line says what was billed earlier ("up to 29.02.2024 ... in an earlier statement"), and "Advance payments from 01.03.2024" shows from when the advance payments count. Binding terms (statement, billed until, days rented, advance payment) are used correctly. WinAnsi typography is fine (’ and NBSP before %). The weak spots are tense ("are already billed" where English needs "have already been billed"), two ambiguous phrases in the heating lines ("billed months", "within it") and the stiff "entered for the rental contract". The UI's «Billed until» guillemets follow the house style of en.json (128 uses), so they are not raised here.
