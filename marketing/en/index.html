Open source · Free · Made for Switzerland

Your utility cost statement, made easier.

Dwello prepares the utility cost statement for private landlords. Drop in the invoices, check the statement, send it with a QR-bill. The software runs on your own machine, and the AI reads your supporting documents on your own computer or, if you set it up that way, at a provider of your choice.

Your landlord year

Four steps. Once a year. No spreadsheets.

Dwello is made for people who own a building and do not want to run a property management business. The overview tells you what to do next, and every field explains its term.

  1. Once, at the start

    Add your building

    Search the address, add the apartments with area and rooms, add tenants and rental contracts with the agreed utility costs and advance payment.

  2. All year

    Upload invoices

    Drop in the PDFs. The AI reads amount, date and category and tells you what to check.

  3. After the period ends

    Create statements

    For the whole building at once, to the day when a tenant changes, and with a heating profile. Dwello deducts the agreed advance payments; missing payments are settled outside the statement.

  4. Right after

    Check and send

    By email or by letter, with a Swiss QR-bill and in your tenants’ language.

Invoices and AI

Invoices by drag and drop. Read on your own computer.

Drop up to 20 invoices into Dwello at once. The AI reads the invoice issuer, amount, date and service period and assigns the category. Whatever it is not sure about, Dwello points out. The invoice is only created once you confirm.

  1. You drop in an invoice, say the one from the water utility.
  2. The AI reads the amount, date and category.
  3. You check the details and confirm.
  • Local AI with Ollama, no cloudWith the local AI your supporting documents never leave the server. If you like, connect any OpenAI-compatible AI instead.
  • Missing invoices flaggedDwello expects what came last year. If the water invoice is missing, you see it before the statement.
  • Allocable or notCosts you may not pass on to tenants stay out of the statement.
Utility cost statement

A statement that explains every number.

Dwello calculates by the rules of Swiss tenancy law and shows, for each category, the building’s costs, the apartment share and the days rented. Your tenants see the same breakdown in the PDF: Hans Müller in apartment 1.01 of Musterhaus Zentrum, for example, owes CHF 215.80 for 2025. Check every statement before you send it: Dwello is no substitute for legal advice.

  • Only what the contract allows Art. 257a COOnly the utility costs agreed in the rental contract are billed.
  • To the day for tenant changes and vacancyThe landlord bears the utility costs of a vacant apartment.
  • Rounded to 5 centimes, in the tenant’s favourAmounts under CHF 5 are neither claimed nor paid out right away but carried over to the next statement. The final statement after move-out settles every amount.
Distribution keys and heating profiles

Shared fairly, even in winter.

Costs by area, rooms, units or a custom formula, say 70 % by area and 30 % per apartment. Heating costs follow a heating profile: a day rented in January counts more than one in summer. One profile each is included for the Central Plateau, the mountains and Ticino, plus one for heating with hot water.

An example from the demo data: the lift in Musterhaus Zentrum costs CHF 1'690.00 a year. Split per unit, each of the six apartments bears CHF 281.67, Hans Müller’s apartment 1.01 included.

Swiss QR-bill and reminders

Your tenants pay with their phone. You see who still owes.

Every statement is a PDF with a payment part to the Swiss standard, in the tenant’s language. If a payment does not arrive, Dwello writes the payment reminder and the reminders, each with its own QR payment part.

  • QR payment part to the SIX standardScan, check, pay. No typing in IBAN and reference.
  • In the tenant’s languageMarco Bernasconi in Lucerne gets his statement in Italian, Nina Brunner hers in English.
  • Payment reminder, 2nd and 3rd reminderBy email or letter. All PDFs of a building also as a ZIP.
Buildings and apartments

Privacy

Built for the Swiss Data Protection Act.

Tenant data is personal data. Dwello treats it that way: it stays on your server, nothing comes to us, and you can answer any information request at any time. Only what a task needs goes out: sign-in with Google, the address search in the federal building register, your e-mails to tenants and, if you set it up that way, invoices to an external AI. You remain responsible for your tenants’ data.

  • Your server, your databaseDwello runs on your PC, your NAS or with a host of your choice, including a data centre in Switzerland.
  • Invoices stay at homeThe AI runs with Ollama on your server. No invoice goes to a cloud service unless you want it to.
  • Data portability at the push of a buttonArt. 28 FADP. All data of one tenant as a ZIP, with folders in their language.
  • No trackingNo ads, no analytics cookies. We never learn that you use Dwello.
  • Log and privacy statementEvery change to statement data is logged. A privacy statement for your tenants is included.

Install

Have it set up, then work in the browser.

Someone who knows computers sets it up once: a family member, a friend or your IT service. Give that person the guide; it takes about an hour. After that you work in the browser, with no technical knowledge. That person does updates and regular backups; the guide describes them.

Guide to hand on
Terminal · Windows, macOS, Linux
# 1. Download Dwello
$ git clone https://github.com/rudini/Dwello.git && cd Dwello
# 2. Create the settings, then fill them in (guide)
$ cp .env.example .env
# 3. Start: app, database and local AI
$ docker compose -f docker-compose.prod.yml up -d
# 4. Load the AI model (once, about 6 GB)
$ docker exec dwello-ollama ollama pull qwen3-vl:8b-instruct
✓ Dwello is running at http://localhost:3000

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need technical knowledge?

For the setup, and after that for updates and regular backups. Ask someone who knows computers and give them the guide. To work with Dwello you need no prior knowledge: every term is explained at its field.

Is Dwello really free?

Yes. Dwello is open source and free. At most you pay for your own server or the power for your PC, and the postage for letters. If you use an external AI instead of the local one, you pay the provider directly.

How many apartments is Dwello suited for?

For private landlords with one to five buildings and up to 50 apartments. Dwello is not made for large property management firms with teams.

What happens if the AI misreads an invoice?

Nothing happens without your confirmation. The AI suggests the details and Dwello tells you which ones to check. The invoice is only created once you confirm. You can also add any invoice yourself.

Has Dwello been checked legally?

No, not by a lawyer or an association. Dwello calculates by the Swiss Code of Obligations and the Tenancy Ordinance (VMWG), and automated tests check the rules from them that Dwello applies. That does not replace legal advice: in a dispute, the homeowners’ association or the conciliation authority can help you.

Can I try Dwello without installing anything?

Yes. The demo at demo.dwello.ch shows three buildings in Zurich, Lucerne and Interlaken with 13 apartments. It is reset every night.
